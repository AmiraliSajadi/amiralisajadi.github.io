About

I’m a computer science PhD candidate at Drexel University, where I’ve been since 2022. I work with Dr. Chatterjee.

My first projects studied interpersonal trust and emotions in developer conversations. My current work focuses on software security and AI, including generated patches, static analysis, and exploitability confirmation.

Teaching and mentoring

I’ve worked with undergraduate researchers on developer communication, software security, and agent visualization. I’ve also been a teaching assistant for software design and introductory computer science courses.
